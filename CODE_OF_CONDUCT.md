# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free
community for everyone who contributes to Awesome Telegram Bots.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Keep discussions focused on the quality and accuracy of the list.
- Disclose affiliations with submitted resources.
- Accept review decisions and requested changes professionally.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Repeated promotional submissions, deceptive claims, or undisclosed conflicts
  of interest.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Disrupting reviews or attempting to bypass contribution requirements.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject, close, or remove contributions and interactions
that violate these standards. Report conduct concerns privately through the
maintainer's [GitHub profile](https://github.com/erkcet). Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as practical.
